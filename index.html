About Nathan

Nathan Rubin is an author, collegiate student-athlete, youth speaker, and storyteller from Boston, MA. He studies Economics, Community Relations, and Diaspora at the Liberal Arts College of the University of Massachusetts - Boston, while competing as a men's basketball player.

Across his published children’s books, Chasing a Dream and The Elephant’s Garden, Nathan writes to inspire confidence, emotional resilience, and self-belief in young readers. He believes stories have the power to plant seeds of strength and joy, helping children see themselves as heroes in their own lives.

Whether on the basketball court, reading to elementary students, leading a community workshop, or conducting economic research, Nathan brings an authentic dedication to lifting up young people and serving his community.
